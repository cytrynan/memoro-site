memoro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · 日本語版

This is an English translation of the Japanese policy. If the two versions differ, the Japanese version is the one that applies.

Summary

memoro ("the extension") is a Chrome extension designed with your privacy as the first priority. It never collects or transmits the flashcards you create or your study data. The only time it communicates with an outside service is when you buy or restore the one-time unlock, and then only with ExtensionPay / Stripe to process the payment.

The purchase feature (including regional pricing), the review timer, and the links to the feedback form are features of version 0.3.0 and later.

Information we collect

We do not collect study data. The extension does not collect the flashcards you create, your review progress, your browsing history, or usage analytics. It uses no cookies and no tracking technology.

  • It does not collect your name, email address, or other personal information for any study feature.
  • It does not read or record your browsing or access history.
  • It does not collect usage or analytics data.

Exception (only if you buy the unlock): If you buy the paid version (a one-time purchase; ¥600 in Japan, shown in your local currency elsewhere), the extension communicates with the payment service ExtensionPay (which uses Stripe) to check your purchase status and to restore a purchase on another device. In that case, the ExtensionPay library stores your purchase status, including the email address you entered at checkout, in chrome.storage.sync (or chrome.storage.local when sync is not available). If Chrome Sync is turned on, this information is synced to your other devices through your Google account (your flashcards themselves stay in chrome.storage.local only and are never synced). The extension never shows or exports this information in its screens, card manager, or export files. See "Operations that communicate with a server" below.

How the regional price is chosen: The price shown on the purchase button is chosen on your device from your device's time zone and language settings. The time zone and language settings themselves are never sent anywhere. Only when you press the purchase button does the name of the chosen price plan (for example, the US dollar plan) go to ExtensionPay so that the checkout page can show the right price.

Where data is stored

The flashcards you create (front and back text, tags, the URL and title of the source page, and review progress) and your settings are stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).

  • The extension never sends cards or study data outside your browser.
  • No third party, including the developer, can access this data.
  • Only http and https source URLs are stored; URLs in any other scheme are discarded when the card is saved.
  • Review timer settings (whether notifications are on, the notification time, and whether today's notification has already been decided) are also stored only in your browser.

Operations that communicate with a server (purchase feature)

The extension can communicate with ExtensionPay (Stripe handles the payment itself) to process a purchase. This happens only during the operations listed below. Everything else, including installing the extension, creating cards, reviewing, managing cards, and export/import (JSON and CSV), involves no network communication at all.

Operation Network
Installing the extension, creating, reviewing, editing, or deleting cards, export/import (JSON and CSV) None
Pressing the purchase button on the settings page (in the Japanese UI the label is "600円で無制限にする（買い切り）", which means "Unlock unlimited cards for ¥600 (one-time)") Yes (fetching plan information, opening the checkout page, checking purchase status)
Pressing "Already purchased?" on the settings page (to restore a purchase made on another device) Yes
Pressing "Recheck purchase status" on the settings page Yes
Anything else (including normal use after a purchase) None

The extension does not check your license in the background. Unless you perform one of the operations above, it never contacts ExtensionPay.

Links to external pages (feedback form and store reviews)

When you press "Send feedback", "Share your thoughts", or "Write a review on the store" on the settings page or in the popup, a Google Form or the Chrome Web Store review page opens in a new browser tab. The extension itself sends nothing to these pages. The feedback form URL carries only the extension's version number (for example, 0.3.0) so that we can read your answer in context; it never includes card content or personal information. Anything you type and submit on those pages is handled under the privacy policy of the page's operator (Google).

Sharing with third parties

We do not share or sell study data (such as card content) with anyone. Only during a purchase does the extension communicate with the following payment processors.

Processor Purpose Privacy policy
ExtensionPay LLC Payment platform for browser extensions extensionpay.com/privacy.html
Stripe, Inc. Credit card processing (through ExtensionPay) stripe.com/privacy

Why each permission is used

Permission Purpose
storage To store your flashcards and settings (including purchase status) in your browser
contextMenus To show the right-click menu item "Add a card to memoro"
activeTab / scripting To show the save dialog temporarily in the current tab when you use the right-click menu, the keyboard shortcut, or the popup button
alarms To refresh the "due today" count periodically (at most once an hour) and to schedule the review timer notification
notifications (optional) Only if you turn on the review timer's notifications, to show "You have N cards to review today" once a day at the time you set. No notification appears on days with no due cards, and notifications never include card content (front or back)

The extension requests no host_permissions. Communication with the payment server for the purchase feature works without any additional permanent site access. None of the permissions is used to send study data anywhere.

notifications is an optional permission. It is not requested at install time; Chrome asks for it only when you turn on the review timer's notifications. Notifications are shown on your device by Chrome, and no network communication is involved. Turn notifications off and no further notifications appear.

How to delete your data

  • Individual cards: delete them from the extension's settings page (card manager).
  • Purchase status: removed when you uninstall the extension from Chrome. If it was stored in chrome.storage.sync, Chrome Sync has also copied it to your other synced devices, so to remove it completely you must uninstall the extension on every device or delete the data from Chrome's sync settings.
  • All data: uninstalling the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored (study data and purchase status).

Children's privacy

Because the extension collects no study data, it can be used safely at any age. Buying the paid version requires a payment method such as a credit card, with a parent's or guardian's consent.
