Get started in 3 steps

Select. Write. Review.

memoro is a flashcard maker for web pages: select text, choose “Add a card to memoro” from the right-click menu, then save it in the dialog. Then spend a few minutes a day on the review due today, shown on the toolbar icon.

  1. 1

    Select

    Select text you want to remember on a page, then right-click → “Add a card to memoro”.

  2. 2

    Write

    Enter a short question in the dialog and save. You can leave it blank; the selected text will become the front.

  3. 3

    Review

    Click the “Due today” badge on the toolbar icon, then rate yourself with “Again / Good / Easy”.

Main features

Review only what’s due today.

Spaced repetition (SM-2) selects only the cards whose review date has come and shows them to you. It is suited to retaining knowledge you want to keep beyond the first read, such as certification exam terms, English vocabulary, and specialized knowledge for work.

Review timer and the forgetting curve

How long until
your next review.

We gradually forget what we learn as time passes. Psychologist Hermann Ebbinghaus measured this “forgetting curve” on himself in 1885, and a 2015 replication study reported results closely resembling his original data. memoro sets each card’s next review date with spaced repetition (the SM-2 method), which widens the interval after every review, and the review timer tells you when it is due.

  • Review timer

    The popup shows how long until your next review, along with the date and the number of cards. The “Manage cards” screen also shows the next review for each card.

  • One reminder a day (optional)

    At the time you set (21:00 by default), a notification appears only on days when cards are due. It shows only the number of cards, never their content.

  • Memory retention chart

    A schematic drawn from the average of your cards, showing how review intervals widen. It is not measured data, but an illustration of how spaced repetition works.

Today's results when you finish a review

When you finish a review and have recalled at least one card, the completion screen shows the number of cards you recalled today. When you recalled cards that you had also recalled last time, it also shows the average number of days since you last saw them and how much the days until next review have increased (for example, 4 → 11 days on average), and draws two illustrative forgetting curves in one chart, with and without today's review (the curves are illustrative, not a measurement of your memory).

Tools to help you recall

  • Hint

    If you cannot recall an answer, press “Hint” to reveal it gradually from the beginning (a few characters at a time in Japanese, one word at a time in languages such as English; the full answer is never revealed). After a hint, “Easy” is treated the same as “Good” so the interval does not grow too much.

  • How “retained” is counted

    A card counts as “retained” after three successive successful recalls on three separate days following the day you first recalled it. This count appears on the completion screen and in the card list (we use a relearning procedure from memory research as a guide).

  • Review before bed

    The default review reminder time is 21:00. A study suggests that sleeping within a few hours after learning helps retention, so we chose a time before bed (you can change it in settings).

  • Sources: H. Ebbinghaus, Über das Gedächtnis (1885) / English translation Memory, Chapter VII (Ruger & Bussenius, 1913)
  • J. M. J. Murre & J. Dros, “Replication and Analysis of Ebbinghaus’ Forgetting Curve”, PLOS ONE 10(7), 2015
  • P. A. Wozniak, Optimization of learning (1990) — Algorithm SM-2

Privacy

Your card content.
Only on your device.

  • Card content is never sent externally

    Your cards and learning progress are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and are never sent to external servers.

  • Communication only during purchase

    memoro communicates with ExtensionPay / Stripe for payment only when you purchase or restore the one-time unlock (including rechecking your purchase status). All other operations, including card creation, review, card management, and import/export, involve no communication.

  • No account required

    No sign-up or login needed (payment information is required only when purchasing).

  • No ads or tracking.

Permissions (kept to a minimum) Here are the permissions memoro requests and what they are used for. It does not collect browsing history or continuously access all sites.

storage To save your cards and settings (including purchase status) in your browser.
contextMenus To display “Add a card to memoro” in the right-click menu.
activeTab / scripting To show the save dialog in the current tab only when you click the menu item, press the keyboard shortcut, or click “Turn the selected text into a card” in the popup (no continuous access to all sites).
alarms To refresh the badge showing the “Due today” count periodically (at most once per hour) and to schedule the review timer’s reminder time.
notifications (optional) Only if you turn on review timer reminders, to show “N due for review today” at the time you set (Chrome asks for permission when you turn it on; notifications never include card content).

Note: Do not store confidential information or secrets such as passwords in cards. Data is stored locally without encryption.
